Start

Goal and baseline

Replace gsplat's per-Gaussian optimization with a point-conditioned network: LitePT plus a thin per-point head predicts every Gaussian's scale, rotation, opacity and SH colour from its position, f(mean_i) → (scale_i, quat_i, opacity_i, sh0_i, shN_i). The means are a fixed input; the only trainable weights are the network's, trained end to end through gsplat's rasterizer with L1 + SSIM (λ = 0.2). Open question from the start: a per-scene reparameterization, or a prior that works across scenes.

Reference: gsplat MCMC baselines on Mip-NeRF 360 (30k steps, eval split):

1Step 1 001–003

LitePT on bonsai, colours only, no augmentation

Can a point transformer predict the Gaussians of one scene from fixed positions? The input points are the means of a converged MCMC checkpoint (1M points on bonsai) and the only per-point feature is the colour. The network predicts every Gaussian's scale, rotation, opacity and SH colour. Numbers for 003 come from wandb.

Result bonsai 28.75 dB in 10k steps (MCMC: 32.80).

Observation 1 001–002

The point spacing sets the LitePT voxel size

2026-09 · points-per-voxel analysis

Change

scripts/001_points_per_voxel.py histograms how many Gaussian centres (opacity > 0.005) fall in each voxel of an MCMC checkpoint for a list of voxel sizes; 002_points_per_voxel_mipnerf.sh runs it on every Mip-NeRF scene for voxel sizes 0.001–0.1. LitePT's grid_size is the same knob.

Findings

  • Voxel size 0.005 (normalized scene units) was chosen and kept in every later run.
  • On the four indoor scenes the median nearest-neighbour distance is 0.0010–0.0015, i.e. 2.2–4.1 points per 0.005 voxel (kitchen densest), so one shared voxel size works across them.

The histograms themselves are not on this cluster; the numbers are from the 007 script header.

2Step 2 004–006

4Step 4 009–014

Switch to EDGS initialisation

MCMC means need a converged per-scene optimization first. From 009 on, the input points come from EDGS (RoMa dense matches triangulated from the training views, every 8th view held out), with RoMa features per point; then more scenes and the Muon optimizer. Numbers from the Slurm logs and wandb.

Observation 16 016

Positional encoding speeds up training, then eval collapses

2026-10-14 · 016 — Fourier positional encoding of the input points

Change

015 (offsets 1.5 vox) + positional encoding of the input points (models/pos_encoding.py, scripts/016_train_edgs_muon_offsets_pe.sh):

  • center = scene origin (with data.parser.normalize, the median of the COLMAP points; augmentation rotates/scales about it) — no per-batch center;
  • one isotropic divider r = 95th percentile of |coord|, computed after augmentation → p = R x / r (rotates with the cloud, invariant to the augmentation's scale). Bonsai: r95 = 3.56 normalized units, max |p| ≈ 46, 0.22 % of points beyond 1.5 r;
  • encoding [p | sin/cos(2^k π p)], k < 4 = 27 channels;
  • two injection points:
    • input: concatenated after the RoMa MLP, [rgb | mlp(RoMa) | PE], LitePT in_channels 67 → 94 — job 504, wandb pdbb1nad;
    • embed: added after LitePT's stem through a zero-initialized Linear(27 → 36) — job 505, wandb 3gglxh35.
step015016 input016 embed
1k19.0820.0520.02
2k20.2613.1819.36
3k19.9514.8820.98
4k20.6413.3015.93

Both cancelled at ~4k (checkpoints up to step 3999 kept).

Findings

  • PE speeds up early training: +1 dB over 015 at 1k, both variants alike.
  • Then both collapse on eval (input from 2k, embed at 4k) while the training PSNR stays healthy (~21.5, ahead of 015) and training-time scales are normal (max ≈ 0.1, same as 015).
  • The collapse is a train / eval mode mismatch: the same step-1999 checkpoint of input gives 12.86 dB rendered in eval mode but 20.65 dB in train mode (same weights, pose and inputs); embed 19.36 vs 20.35. The only mode-dependent layer is DropPath (model.drop_path=0.3, active only in the attention stages 3–4; no BatchNorm with model.norm=layer, dropouts 0).
  • Hypothesis (not verified): with absolute position available, the deep branches learn position-specific corrections tuned to the stochastic (dropped, ×1/keep) training forward; with all branches on in eval they overshoot. Without PE (015) this doesn't happen. embed collapses later because its PE path starts at zero.
  • Suspected amplifier: the unbounded raw-p tail (|p| up to ~46 entering the stem conv next to O(1) inputs) → tested in the next two entries.

Observation 19 512

Drop-path causes the eval collapse

2026-10-15 · 016 with raw p, clamp r98, no drop-path
