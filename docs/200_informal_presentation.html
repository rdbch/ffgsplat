4Experiment 4

Integrating Muon

Setup bonsai, EDGS means + RoMa features, 20k steps, aug, LayerNorm; AdamW → Muon (moonlight) on the hidden Linear weights of LitePT and the feature MLP, AdamW on the rest, LR 2e-3

Results
  • 22.07 dB PSNR with Muon vs 21.49 dB with AdamW at 20k (+0.58 dB; SSIM 0.770 vs 0.755, LPIPS 0.393 vs 0.409) - Bonsai
  • 1.8× speedup: Muon reaches AdamW's final 21.49 dB at 11k steps instead of 20k, i.e. 4.8 h instead of 8.5 h (a Muon step costs ~2% more)
  • Muon is ahead at every eval, by 0.3–1.6 dB

5Experiment 5

Adding positional encodings

Setup bonsai, EDGS means + RoMa features, Muon, offsets 1.5 vox, aug, LayerNorm, 20k steps; + Fourier PE of the input points (after augmentation), concatenated to the features; drop-path 0

Results
  • PE speeds up early training: +0.9 dB over no PE at 1k, +1.2 dB at 3k
  • Final gain is small: 22.59 dB PSNR vs 22.36 without PE (+0.23 dB, SSIM 0.789 vs 0.783, LPIPS 0.373 vs 0.378) - Bonsai, and only with sin/cos alone; adding the raw p channels ends equal to no PE (22.34)
  • Needs drop-path 0 (see the pitfall below)
  • PE is a scene-absolute input: the gain is not expected to carry over to unseen scenes or to chunks

Pitfall: eval collapse

With LitePT's default drop-path 0.3, every PE variant collapses on eval after 2–3k steps (e.g. 20.05 → 13.18 dB) while the training PSNR stays healthy. The same checkpoint gives 12.86 dB rendered in eval mode and 20.65 dB in train mode: absolute position + drop-path is a train / eval mismatch. Bounding the encoding (no raw p, or a radial clamp) only delays it; drop-path 0 removes it. Without PE (015) it does not happen.

Caveat: 015 ran with drop-path 0.3 and there is no 015 at drop-path 0, so the +0.23 dB mixes PE with drop-path.

Optimisation: frustum culling
Why
  • Today every step passes the whole scene to LitePT: 1M (bounded) / 1.25M (unbounded) points on DL3DV, 1M–1.5M on Mip-NeRF, so memory grows with the scene: ~80 GB allocated / ~100 GB reserved on a full H200 for 018
  • A step only renders its 8 views: points that none of them sees get (almost) no gradient anyway
  • Next: cull to the union of the step's view frustums, with a margin for large Gaussians and LitePT's context, before the forward; render as today
